# art-15 — Accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity

**Framework:** [[eu-ai-act]]

Article 15 requires that high-risk AI systems shall be designed and developed in such a way
that they achieve, in the light of their intended purpose, an appropriate level of accuracy,
robustness and cybersecurity, and perform consistently in those respects throughout their
lifecycle.


## Controls

- **art-15.1** — Appropriate level of accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity (mandatory, critical)
- **art-15.2** — Levels of accuracy declared (mandatory, high)
- **art-15.3** — Resilience to errors, faults or inconsistencies (mandatory, critical)
- **art-15.4** — Technical and organisational measures for cybersecurity (mandatory, critical)
- **art-15.5** — Measures to prevent and control biases (mandatory, high)
