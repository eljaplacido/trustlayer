# art-9 — Risk management system

**Framework:** [[eu-ai-act]]

Article 9 requires providers of high-risk AI systems to establish, implement, document,
and maintain a risk management system throughout the entire lifecycle of the AI system.


## Controls

- **art-9.1** — Identification and analysis of known and foreseeable risks (mandatory, critical)
- **art-9.2** — Estimation and evaluation of risks that may emerge (mandatory, critical)
- **art-9.3** — Analysis of post-market monitoring data (mandatory, high)
- **art-9.4** — Adoption of suitable risk management measures (mandatory, critical)
- **art-9.5** — Testing procedures (mandatory, critical)
- **art-9.6** — Assessment of compliance with requirements (mandatory, high)
- **art-9.7** — Evaluation of residual risk (mandatory, critical)
- **art-9.8** — Documentation of risk management system (mandatory, high)
