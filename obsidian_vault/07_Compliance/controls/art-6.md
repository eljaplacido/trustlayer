# art-6 — Risk classification rules

**Framework:** [[eu-ai-act]]

Article 6 establishes the risk classification rules. AI systems are classified as:
- Prohibited (Art. 5): Unacceptable risk
- High-risk (Art. 6 + Annex III): Significant risk to health, safety, or fundamental rights
- Limited-risk: Transparency obligations (Art. 52)
- Minimal-risk: No specific obligations


## Controls

- **art-6.1** — Risk classification assessment (mandatory, critical)
- **art-6.2** — Intended purpose definition (mandatory, critical)
