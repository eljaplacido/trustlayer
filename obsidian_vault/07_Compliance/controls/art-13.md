# art-13 — Transparency and provision of information to deployers

**Framework:** [[eu-ai-act]]

Article 13 requires that high-risk AI systems shall be designed and developed in such a way
as to ensure that their operation is sufficiently transparent to enable deployers to
interpret the system's output and use it appropriately.


## Controls

- **art-13.1** — Sufficient transparency for interpretation (mandatory, critical)
- **art-13.2** — Instructions for use (mandatory, critical)
- **art-13.3** — Information on intended purpose and limitations (mandatory, critical)
- **art-13.4** — Information on performance and accuracy (mandatory, high)
