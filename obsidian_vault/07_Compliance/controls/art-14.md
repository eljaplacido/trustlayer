# art-14 — Human oversight

**Framework:** [[eu-ai-act]]

Article 14 requires that high-risk AI systems shall be designed and developed in such a way,
including with appropriate human-machine interface tools, as to ensure effective human
oversight by natural persons.


## Controls

- **art-14.1** — Human-machine interface tools for oversight (mandatory, critical)
- **art-14.2** — Measures to enable human oversight (mandatory, critical)
- **art-14.3** — Proportionality of oversight measures (mandatory, high)
- **art-14.4** — Assignment of human oversight to natural persons (mandatory, high)
