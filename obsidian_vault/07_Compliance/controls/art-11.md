# art-11 — Technical documentation

**Framework:** [[eu-ai-act]]

Article 11 requires that the technical documentation of a high-risk AI system shall be
drawn up before that system is placed on the market or put into service and shall be kept
up-to-date.


## Controls

- **art-11.1** — Technical documentation drawn up before placing on market (mandatory, critical)
- **art-11.2** — Demonstration of compliance with requirements (mandatory, critical)
- **art-11.3** — Minimum elements of technical documentation (Annex IV) (mandatory, critical)
