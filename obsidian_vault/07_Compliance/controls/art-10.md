# art-10 — Data and data governance

**Framework:** [[eu-ai-act]]

Article 10 requires that high-risk AI systems which make use of techniques involving the
training of models with data shall be developed on the basis of training, validation and
testing data sets that meet the quality criteria referred to in paragraphs 2 to 5.


## Controls

- **art-10.1** — Training, validation and testing data sets (mandatory, critical)
- **art-10.2** — Data governance and management practices (mandatory, critical)
- **art-10.3** — Examination for biases (mandatory, critical)
- **art-10.4** — Identification of possible data gaps or shortcomings (mandatory, high)
- **art-10.5** — Relevance, representativeness, error-free and completeness (mandatory, critical)
