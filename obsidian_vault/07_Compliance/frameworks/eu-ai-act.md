# eu-ai-act

EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) - Control Framework for High-Risk AI Systems.
This framework covers the key obligations for providers and deployers of high-risk AI systems
under Articles 9-15, with additional context from Article 6 (risk classification).
